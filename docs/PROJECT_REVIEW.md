## What the pass tells us

No content corrections were requested. The seven-part structure, the per-insight confidence gradient
(High / High / Medium / Medium), the disclosed 19% versus 57–59% occupancy conflict, and the refused
market sizing all went unchallenged. The marks came from structure and explicit sourcing.

Both tips are about **framing for an executive**, not about analysis quality:

1. **Boundary conditions over disclaimers.** "What should not be concluded" should read as the limits
   of what the evidence establishes ("relative position within the 18-market peer set, not absolute
   market size") rather than a passive caveat.
2. **Conclusion before evidence.** A 3–4 sentence "Bottom line" box under the title would give the
   executive-first read without reordering the graded sections.

"Post-pass feedback — what the reviewer rewards beyond the rubric".

## Udacity's post-pass refinement list (surfaced after the pass)

The submission page also lists eight improvements, seven of which are structural and cheap:

1. Leadership summary at the top of future briefs.
2. Tie strategic implications to operational milestones or numeric financial triggers.
3. Connect each recommended decision to the specific evidence-supported insight behind it.
4. Make non-conclusions precise by stating what the evidence cannot establish.
5. Keep the leadership summary concise, decision-oriented, and consistent with the stated
   confidence levels and limitations.
6. For Medium/Low confidence insights, outline the specific follow-up research needed to validate.
7. Define a risk mitigation strategy alongside each limitation.
8. Use a standardised source-quality matrix so confidence scoring stays consistent across briefs.

Applied to this submission: items 1 and 2 were the gap (no bottom-line box; triggers existed only in
Appendix E rather than in the implications themselves). Items 3, 4, 5 and 8 were already in place —
§8 cites insight numbers, §8 non-conclusions name what the data cannot support, §9 mirrors the §6
confidence split, and sources were graded on a fixed ladder. Item 6 was partially met (confidence
reasons given, no follow-up study named) and item 7 was not met (limitations were listed without
mitigations).

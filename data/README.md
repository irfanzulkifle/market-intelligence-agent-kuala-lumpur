# Data

Derived tables used to produce every figure in the research brief. All five files are computed from a
single public dataset; none of the numbers in `BRIEF.md` were generated by a language model, and each
one can be recomputed from these files.

## Files

| File | Rows | What it contains |
|---|---|---|
| `KL_listings_303.csv` | 303 | Listing-level extract, 24 columns: neighbourhood, property type, room type, host portfolio band, superhost flag, annualised ADR, TTM occupancy, TTM revenue, review scores, available days |
| `KL_segment_summary.csv` | 4 | Metrics aggregated by host-portfolio band (casual, small, mid, professional): listing count, median ADR, median occupancy, median TTM revenue |
| `KL_peer_benchmark_18_markets.csv` | 18 | ADR, occupancy and RevPAR for 18 APAC short-term rental markets, from the same source, for like-for-like comparison with Kuala Lumpur |
| `KL_monthly_market_series.csv` | 12 | Monthly ADR, occupancy and revenue for Kuala Lumpur, February 2025 to January 2026 |
| `KL_correlations.csv` | 6 | Pairwise correlations, including the ADR-versus-occupancy coefficient (r = −0.011) that anchors Insight 2 |

## Conventions

- Monetary values in the listing-level and segment tables are **USD**; the source dataset converts at
  approximately 4.31 MYR/USD for this period. External hotel benchmarks quoted in the brief are in MYR
  and are labelled as such.
- `occupancy` is occupied nights as a share of nights available, computed by the data provider. The
  brief discloses that market-wide hotel occupancy for the same period is 57–59% and treats the gap as
  a measurement-definition difference rather than a market signal. Do not compare the two directly.
- `available_days` counts nights **not** booked, following the provider's schema.
- The sample is 303 listings drawn from a market of roughly 20,000–24,000 active units. Absolute levels
  are indicative; relative comparisons within the sample are the only defensible use.
